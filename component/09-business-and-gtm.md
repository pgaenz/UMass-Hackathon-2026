Non-code deliverables that make the pitch land with business/industry judges. Frameworks
from Prof. Todaro's ESM lectures and the pitch deck blueprint.

### `C-BIZ-01` Problem & Research Evidence Base

- **Purpose:** Back the three problem pillars with verified, cited research.
- **Responsibilities:** Curate and fact-check sources: WHO loneliness deaths (871k/year),
  Surgeon General (+26–30% mortality, 15 cigarettes/day), U-M poll (34% isolated / 37% lack
  companionship), JAMA purpose & mortality (2.43x), APA cognition shield, Cigna (67% Gen Z
  lonely), Harvard Making Caring Common (75% lack meaning), Stanford age segregation.
- **Inputs → Outputs:** Sources → footnoted problem slide + Q&A cheat sheet.
- **Dependencies:** —
- **Phase:** B
- **Acceptance Signals:** Every statistic has a live URL and year; no unsourced claim on the
  problem slides.
- **Risks / Open Questions:** Source freshness; over-claiming causality.

### `C-BIZ-02` Market Sizing (TAM / SAM / SOM)

- **Purpose:** Show a credible, defensible market using top-down + bottom-up methods.
- **Responsibilities:** TAM \$57.8B top-down / \$48.0B bottom-up SaaS; SAM \$4.2B; SOM
  \$21M–\$42M ARR (3–5 yrs); Year-1 Boston beachhead \$1.2M ARR / 10k seats; methodology
  notes and assumptions.
- **Inputs → Outputs:** Industry data + WHO demographics → funnel slide + model sheet.
- **Dependencies:** `C-BIZ-01`
- **Phase:** B
- **Acceptance Signals:** Math is reproducible from stated assumptions; top-down and
  bottom-up converge plausibly; sources cited (Fortune Business Insights, WHO, Census).
- **Risks / Open Questions:** \$120 ARPU assumption; seat-based vs. household pricing.

---

### `C-BIZ-03` Competitive Analysis & Positioning

- **Purpose:** Map direct/indirect competitors and articulate the wedge.
- **Responsibilities:** 4-step framework (Identify → Gather → Analyze → Insights); direct
  (StoryWorth/Remento/Storii/Tell Mel, Papa) and indirect (TikTok/YouTube/Spotify, senior
  centers/penpal nonprofits); positioning statement + differentiators.
- **Inputs → Outputs:** Competitor research → matrix slide + positioning statement.
- **Dependencies:** `C-BIZ-01`
- **Phase:** B
- **Acceptance Signals:** At least 5 competitors with strengths/weaknesses and pricing;
  positioning is one sentence and unique; "why now" articulated.
- **Risks / Open Questions:** Competitors shipping similar AI features; brand-new entrant status.

---

### `C-BIZ-04` Freemium Model & Unit Economics

- **Purpose:** Define monetization and prove the economics work.
- **Responsibilities:** Tiers (Community Free; Plus \$9.99/mo or \$99/yr; Institutional
  \$499/mo), CAC \$25, LTV \$180, LTV:CAC 7.2x, 24-month lifetime; B2C + B2B2C mechanics.
- **Inputs → Outputs:** Pricing strategy → tiers slide + unit-economics table.
- **Dependencies:** `C-BIZ-02/03`
- **Phase:** B
- **Acceptance Signals:** CAC/LTV assumptions stated and internally consistent; free tier
  drives awareness without cannibalizing paid; B2B price justified.
- **Risks / Open Questions:** Senior willingness to pay; institutional sales cycle.

---

### `C-BIZ-05` GTM & "Crossing the Chasm" Roadmap

- **Purpose:** Show a realistic path from beachhead to scale.
- **Responsibilities:** Phase 1 Boston beachhead (UMass Boston + MA senior living, 1,000
  users, \$150K ARR run-rate); Phase 2 national B2B + cross-lingual dubbing (\$1.2M → \$5.5M
  ARR); Phase 3 healthcare "social prescription" + global archive (200k+ subs, \$25M+ ARR);
  Innovators → Early Adopters → Early Majority.
- **Inputs → Outputs:** Strategy → roadmap slide + milestone table.
- **Dependencies:** `C-BIZ-02/03/04`
- **Phase:** B
- **Acceptance Signals:** Each phase has a metric, channel and partnership target; chasm
  crossing is explicit; Year-1 milestones align with the SOM.
- **Risks / Open Questions:** Healthcare reimbursement timeline; multi-language scope.
